# Conflict resolution

This policy covers disputes about this repository: code, documentation, releases, and how people are treated in project channels. It follows the conflict rule in the Apereo incubation process, section 4.12 of the copy published at `http://archived.apereo.org/content/apereo-incubation-process` (retrieved 5 October 2026): consensus first, by communication and negotiation; if that fails, mediation by someone who is not a party to the dispute.

## Where a dispute is raised

Use a public channel:

- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/TheWootini/booklooky-official-lrs-rater/issues) for a defect, change, or process dispute
- [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/TheWootini/booklooky-official-lrs-rater/discussions) for an open question that is not a defect

Harassment and private personal information follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). A conduct report about the Project Lead is handled under “When the Project Lead is a party” below, not by the Project Lead reviewing it alone.

## Steps

1. The people involved state the dispute in the public channel, including the decision they want.
2. They negotiate there. The aim is a consensus they can both live with.
3. If they do not reach consensus, they ask a mediator who is not a party to the dispute and who has not advocated for either outcome. The mediator’s job is to help them reach a decision, not to take over the project.
4. The outcome is written down. Technical, licensing, and product-boundary outcomes go in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).

Routine bug fixes and documentation corrections are not disputes. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) still applies: changes to core rating methodology are not accepted through this path.

## When the Project Lead is a party

Jon Penneman is the only committer and the Project Lead. He does not mediate a dispute in which he is a party, and he does not close that dispute by declaring his own view final.

Until this project has an Apereo mentor or another external institutional representative, step 3 waits for a mediator who is outside the dispute. Once the project is in Apereo incubation, that mediator should be an institutional representative who is external to the case, as section 4.12 describes. This file does not name that person. None has been appointed.

## What does not belong here

- Security reports. Those stay private. See [SECURITY.MD](SECURITY.MD).
- BookLooky.com accounts, billing, and catalog ratings. Those go to the contact form on [booklooky.com](https://booklooky.com).
