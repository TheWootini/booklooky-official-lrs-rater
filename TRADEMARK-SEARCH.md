# Federal trademark knockout search

**Search date:** 5 October 2026  
**Database:** USPTO Trademark Search (`https://tmsearch.uspto.gov`)  
**Method:** POST `https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/prod-v1-0-0/tmsearch`  
**What this is:** A knockout search of the federal register for the exact strings below. It is not a full clearance search. It does not cover state trademarks, common-law use, design marks, or every similar spelling. It is not legal advice.

## What this search does not do

It does not say these names are free to register. Other people use “LRS” for live software and services. LOOKYBOOK was a registered book-related mark and is now cancelled. A filing decision still needs a trademark attorney and a broader search than this one.
